What goes where

Goal
What you are trying to make or do, in one line. Required. For a birthday party it is the party you want to end up with. For a chapter it is the chapter you want to have written. For a resume it is the job you are going after. For a talk it is what you want the room to leave with.
Product
The thing itself, as far as you have got. Empty is fine — you get the first step instead. For a birthday party that is the plan so far. For a chapter it is the draft. For a resume it is the experience section as it stands. For a talk it is the slides you already have.
Additional context
What changes the answer but has no place in the product. For a birthday party that is the theme, who your kid is and who their friends are. For a chapter it is the outline of the story so far. For a resume it is the skills you want to highlight and the work you want to lead with. For a talk it is the conference and who is in the room.
